## Svelte Discord · launch + 2 · Wed 28 Oct 2026

Post in the showcase channel (svelte.dev/chat). One message, no @mentions.

```
Langsys: Svelte i18n where the English sentence is the key

{$t('Your cart is empty', 'Cart')} registers itself the first time it runs and is drafted in every language you've turned on; a translator refines it later and the fix reaches the running app with no redeploy. <Phrase> keeps a sentence with markup as one phrase, <Translate> covers a whole region (placeholders, alt text and aria labels included), <DontTranslate> keeps brand names verbatim.

MIT on npm (langsys-js-svelte), SvelteKit supported with a server-seeded first render.
1-minute walkthrough: https://youtu.be/y-YbRDjV4Wk
Docs: https://docs.langsys.dev/learn?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=launch_2026&utm_content=svelte-discord
```

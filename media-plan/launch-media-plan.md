## Summary

Nothing goes public before the official launch. On week 1, Monday, 9 videos go out together on YouTube with one pinned launch thread on X. After that, a video or a Short goes out every weekday through week 17, with a two-week break over the holidays.

- **Channels:** the @Langsys YouTube channel (82 videos and Shorts, all uploaded and unlisted), the Langsys account on X and the Langsys LinkedIn page, plus launch-week posts in developer communities (see Other channels).
- **Launch day (week 1, Monday):** the complete starting set: what Langsys is, getting started, the five concepts and connecting an AI assistant.
- **After launch:** Svelte and Translation Manager videos alternate until week 8, so developers and the people who own the copy both get something every week. React follows (weeks 8 to 13), then Vue (weeks 14 to 17). Each Short goes out after the long video it comes from.
- **On X:** we upload video directly to X instead of posting YouTube links, and put the full-video link in the first reply.
- **Timing:** everything is written as a week and a weekday, counted from launch: week 1 is launch week. Videos go public at 9:00 a.m. US Eastern, and the X and LinkedIn posts follow at 9:15 a.m.
- **Holiday break:** two weeks with nothing posted over Christmas and New Year, now from week 9, Thursday to week 11, Wednesday. Once the launch date is set, check the break still lands on the holidays.

## How we post on X

Every YouTube video gets an X post on the day it goes public, but the post carries the video itself, not just a link.

- **Upload video to X directly.** X shows posts with outside links to far fewer people, and a video uploaded to X plays on its own in the feed.
- **Shorts:** post the vertical file as it is. They are under 15 seconds and fit X without editing.
- **Long videos:** post the captioned 30 to 60 second clip cut for it. Each drafted X post names the clip to use. The full YouTube link goes in the first reply.
- **Lead with the result, not the code.** Hooks talk about the outcome (your app in every language, no redeploy) and about AI agents doing the work where the video shows it. No "stop writing X" or code snippets as the hook.
- **One post per publishing day.** X follows the YouTube calendar: same day, same video.

The X drafts follow this plan: the launch thread and all 35 daily posts, each naming the Short or clip to upload and the YouTube link for its reply.

## Launch-day runbook

These steps are done with the YouTube publishing tool, on the computer that is signed in to the Langsys channel.

**Once the launch date is set**

1. Move every video's date so the calendar starts on launch Monday, keeping the gaps between videos.
2. Check the new dates.
3. Update the dates on the X posts and rebuild the calendar page.

**On launch day**

1. Switch the link in every YouTube description to "Start free: https://langsys.dev".
2. Do a practice run first: it lists what would go public without changing anything, and should show the 9 launch videos.
3. Make them and their playlists public.
4. Post the X launch thread and pin it.

**Every publishing day after that**

1. Do the practice run, then make that day's videos public.
2. Post that day's X post.

A missed day is caught up the next time videos are made public.
